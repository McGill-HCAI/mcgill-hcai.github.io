## Speaker: Leo Anthony Celi

Leo Anthony Celi is a physician, researcher, and educator with a focus on the intersection of healthcare and artificial intelligence. He is a principal research scientist at the MIT Institute for Medical Engineering and Science, and an associate professor of medicine at Harvard Medical School. Dr. Celi leads the Laboratory for Computational Physiology at MIT, which conducts research on the application of machine learning and AI in healthcare. He is also a practicing intensivist at the Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston.
